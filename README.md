# Program Performance Analysis for a Digital Skills Training Org.
**Evaluating cohort 1-6 to plan for cohort 7 for AReL(Action for Refugee Life)**

**Disclaimer** No real student, instructor, enrolment or attendance record was used.
The structure follows how AReL's real records looks, but every row was generated for this exercise
The findings below show how the analysis works. They are not conclusions about AReL's actual cohort

## Executive Summary
AReL's Monitoring & Evaluation is planning cohort 7 & needs to know what worked or 
failed in the previous cohorts.
Using MySQL, I analysed enrolment and attendance records to find where and when
students disengage
**Attendance** is weakest on **Friday** and mainly drops about a third of the way through
every course and students who dropout show absence building month to month
rather than stopping suddenly.
These findings lead to recommendations on schedule, mid-course support and record keeping 
for cohort 7.

## Key Highlights
- Friday weakest attendance: 53.7%, Monday - strongest attendance: 63.2%
- Attendance starts at 67%, falls to 53% about a third of the way in course and ends
- Students who eventually dropped when from 29.7% absence in their 1st month to 70.8%
- in the 7th while students who completed stayed between 24% and 26%
